# Profile: Girly / Cute

**Palette**: pastel pink `#FFB3D9`, lavender `#C9A7FF`, mint `#A8F0D4`, baby blue `#A7D8FF`,
cream `#FFF4E0`; glow accent hot pink `#FF5FB1` or gold `#FFD93B`. Panels use soft plum
`#3B2450` instead of navy.
**Shapes & studs**: rounded chunky blocks, hearts/stars/bows as decor, studs on floors in pastel.
**Lighting**: soft bright, pinkish ColorCorrection tint, sparkle particles everywhere.
**UI deltas**: rounded tabs, heart/star frame decor, sparkle burst on claim, outfit preview
turntable, rating stars.
**Map deltas**: plaza with café/salon/boutique storefronts, runway or showcase stage, garden.
**Model deltas**: cute faces (big eyes, blush), accessories as separate meshes for mix & match.
**Systems**: Inventory (outfits/items), Currency, Quests/Rewards, Monetization, Trading,
DataStore, Tutorial.
**Never**: harsh red/black palettes, aggressive VFX, scary creatures.
