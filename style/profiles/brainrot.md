# Profile: Brainrot

**Palette**: house palette at full saturation + one "toxic" glow accent per creature family
(lime `#A8F03C`, hot pink `#FF3EC8`, cyan `#3EF0FF`). Plot bases pastel-stud floors with bold
team colors.
**Shapes & studs**: chunky blocky bodies with absurd proportions (giant heads, tiny legs, objects
fused with animals — shark-in-sneakers energy). Studs on every plot floor, conveyor and pedestal.
**Lighting**: bright noon, high bloom on Mythic/Secret auras.
**UI deltas**: meme-y angled tags ("SO RARE!!", "STOLEN!"), giant shake-in money pops,
character name plates in heavy caps with emoji-like stickers.
**Map deltas**: central conveyor/spawn lane where creatures walk past for purchase → ring of
player plots (8 typical) with gates/lasers → open center for chaos. Each plot: display pedestal
grid + income collector pad.
**Model deltas**: every creature has a distinct silhouette readable at 50 studs; idle loop is
exaggerated (wobble, spin, bounce). Mutations (Gold, Diamond, Rainbow, Lava…) recolor + add
particles, never change silhouette.
**Systems typically used**: Currency, Rarity+Mutation, Plots, Stealing, Inventory, Rebirth,
Monetization, Quests/Rewards, DataStore.
**Audio/VFX mood**: loud, punchy, stingers on rare walk-ins, siren on steal attempts.
**Never**: realistic animals, muted palettes, dark night maps, slow pacing.
