# Genre: Dress-up / Fashion

**Loop**: collect clothing items → enter a themed round → style an outfit in time → players vote →
earn currency + rare items → collection index.
**Systems**: Inventory (outfits), Currency, Rarity (mystery boxes + pity), Rewards, Trading,
Monetization, DataService, Tutorial, Guard (vote rate limits).
**Content v1**: 120+ accessories in 6 categories, 10 round themes, runway stage map.
**Pitfalls**: vote manipulation, outfit previews not matching final avatar, harsh palettes.
