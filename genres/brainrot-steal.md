# Genre: Brainrot / Steal-a-Thing

**Loop**: creatures walk down a central conveyor → buy with Cash → they sit on your plot pedestals
and generate Cash/sec → buy rarer ones → steal from other plots / lock your base → rebirth.
**Systems**: Plots, Currency (income), Rarity + Mutation, Inventory (pedestal capacity), Stealing,
Rebirth, Upgrades (pedestal slots, lock time), Monetization, Rewards, DataService, Tutorial, Guard.
**Content v1**: 25–40 creatures across 7 rarities, 6–8 mutations, 8 plots/server, 1 map.
**Pacing**: first purchase < 20s, first Rare < 5 min, first steal attempt < 3 min, rebirth ~45 min.
**Monetization**: x2→x100 Cash ladder, VIP, +pedestal slots, longer lock/shield, server luck,
instant-buy rare walk-ins, starter pack, Season 1 pass.
**Screens**: HUD (cash/sec), shop, index, rebirth, lock button + timer, steal alert banner.
**Pitfalls**: client-side income (exploitable), stolen items duplicating (use Inventory:transfer),
spawn-camping thieves (lock + cooldowns), conveyor spawning on client.
