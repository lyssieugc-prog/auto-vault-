# Genre: Tycoon

**Loop**: claim plot → buy buttons (droppers/conveyors/walls/floors) → income/sec grows → bigger
machines → second floor → rebirth.
**Systems**: Plots, Tycoon, Currency (income), Upgrades, Rebirth, Monetization (gamepass items),
Rewards, DataService (owned buttons), Tutorial, Guard.
**Content v1**: 40–60 buttons, 2 floors, 3 gamepass-gated items, 6–8 plots.
**Pacing**: a button every 10–30s early, every 1–2 min mid, rebirth ~30–45 min.
**Monetization**: x2→x100 Cash ladder, VIP area, auto-collect, faster droppers, instant floor.
**Pitfalls**: buttons appearing with no visible build, income on client, saving every button press.
